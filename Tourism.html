Explore Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is a treasure trove of cultural wonders, historical monuments, and scenic beauty. From the world-renowned Taj Mahal in Agra to the spiritual serenity of Varanasi on the banks of the Ganges, Uttar Pradesh offers travelers a journey through the heart of India's heritage. Join us as we explore the architectural marvels, spiritual sites, and natural landscapes that make Uttar Pradesh a must-visit destination.

Taj Mahal

One of the Seven Wonders of the World, this iconic mausoleum in Agra symbolizes eternal love and is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Varanasi Ghats

The sacred city of Varanasi, with its ancient ghats along the Ganges, offers a deep spiritual experience unlike any other.

Fatehpur Sikri

Explore the grandeur of the Mughal Empire at Fatehpur Sikri, a historical city with impressive architecture and rich history.

Sarnath

The holy site where Lord Buddha gave his first sermon, Sarnath is a place of peace and spiritual significance.

Gorakhpur

Gorakhpur has been the land of ancient glory and medieval mysticism. It is endowed with magnificent monuments / temples of attraction. Gorakhnath Temple , Vishnu Temple, Geeta Vatika, Arogya Mandir, Gita Press and Gita Vatika are the famous places any tourist would

Ayodhya

Ayodhya, also known as Saket, is an ancient city of India, is the birthplace of Bhagwan Shri Ram and setting of the great epic Ramayana. Ayodhya used to be the capital of the ancient Kosala Kingdom
